A dessert-grade blend tuned for cold and sugar. Yame leaves carry an inherent vegetal sweetness; we lean into it with a slightly coarser grind and a sweeter cultivar mix. Reserved for our affogato, soft serve, and warabi mochi — never for koicha. The aim is balance against cream, not against itself.
