A first-flush Samidori from a fifth-generation Uji grower. Twenty-five days under tana shading concentrate the leaf's chlorophyll and amino acids, producing a vivid jade powder with low bitterness and pronounced umami. We grind it the morning we serve it. Best taken as koicha — a thick, almost broth-like pour that asks for attention and rewards it.
