A Yabukita-led single cultivar from Nishio, where the river fog softens the spring light. Shorter shading and a longer drying window pull the leaf toward darker notes — chestnut, cocoa, a low bitterness that hangs on the finish. Forest Deep rewards a cooler pour and a patient sip.
