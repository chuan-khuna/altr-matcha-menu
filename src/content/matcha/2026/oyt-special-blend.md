A special blend sourced from Hoshinomura, one of Yame's most respected tencha-growing villages, shaded for 14 to 21 days before harvest. The O-Y-T™ Special Blend takes a warmer, grounded direction: toasted rice and cereal arrive first, followed by the deeper character of roasted nori and sweet peanut, with a long finish that settles into chocolate cookie. Rich without heaviness — the kind of tea that asks you to slow down.
