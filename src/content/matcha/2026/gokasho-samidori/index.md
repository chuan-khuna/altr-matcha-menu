A limited-edition powder set built around a single Samidori cultivar from Gokasho, Uji — produced by an award-winning grower and ground to competition-grade quality. Each set arrives in a four-colour printed box with a special sticker set and coaster.

Samidori is prized for its Ooika — a distinctively fresh, sweet fragrance that surfaces from careful shading. On the palate it delivers smooth, rounded umami without any harshness, backed by a quiet natural sweetness. The tea has real presence and energy, and the aftertaste lingers long and clean, inviting a slower, more deliberate pace.
