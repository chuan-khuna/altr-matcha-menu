If Uji Okumidori is already made in small amounts, Uji Samidori is made in even fewer. Samidori asks for more care in the field than most cultivars, so each year only a little of the harvest ever becomes matcha.

This single-cultivar Samidori from Uji is one clear-matcha drinkers shouldn't miss — especially anyone drawn to a tea that keeps something back. Its pull is the aftertaste: long, layered, and unlike any other matcha, the kind you keep chasing sip after sip.

We'd point you to it clear, as an Usucha or a Light Brew. It holds up with milk too, but the finish is what you come for.
