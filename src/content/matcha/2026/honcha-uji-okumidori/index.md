Matcha from Uji is best known for its seaweed character. But a few gardens there have developed teas with a nutty side — not roasted, not scorched, just a gentle nuttiness that drinks easily.

This single-cultivar Okumidori from Uji took years to secure. It is one of those nutty Uji teas, and only a little is made each year. The colour is a bright, vivid green, the aroma carries a soft note of nuts, and the umami runs high.

It works just as well clear as it does with milk.
