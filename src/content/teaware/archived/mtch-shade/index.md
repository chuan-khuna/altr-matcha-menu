MTCH's second chawan, and the answer to AIR. Where AIR was an argument about
air — that working air into the bowl as you whisk is what pulls the flavour of
matcha out — SHADE is about the other half of the craft: the weeks of blocked
light before the leaf is ever picked, the shading that gives Japanese tea its
sweetness and its depth. The colour of the bowl is named for it.

Made from 100% polycarbonate: light in the hand, heat-resistant to 141°C, the
same size and volume as AIR, with a handful of details cleaned up along the way.
It draws the sweetness and the umami out quickly, and stops short of the
bitterness and the dry astringency that come with pulling too hard.

Released June 2025.
