Hana is built for milk. We blend a floral Okumidori from Kagoshima with a deeper Asahi from Uji, then balance the grind to hold body against steamed dairy. Stone fruit on the nose, a creamy middle, and just enough astringency to keep the finish honest. Equally at home over ice or under a tight 60° pour.
